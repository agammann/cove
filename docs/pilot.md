# Optional early-user pilot

No pilot results are claimed. Recruiting is not a prerequisite for completing the software.

Recruit a small group of individuals who already switch assistants for a recurring project. Ask each to do one comparable handoff using their normal process and one using Cove. Counterbalance order where practical. Let them choose non-sensitive material; obtain consent before observing any project text. A stopwatch and a short interview are sufficient; avoid recording conversations by default.

Record time preparing context, information the receiving assistant still needs, connection/setup friction, whether participants return for another handoff in the next week, and their stated willingness to pay. Keep copy, retrieval, and actual observed continuation distinct. Report medians and individual variation rather than claiming causal improvement from a small convenience sample. Track manual and connected workflows separately. Ask what users omitted and why they abandoned setup.

An owner-only event export can support repeat-use observations, but two credential IDs do not establish different providers. Do not infer productivity gains or willingness to pay from tool calls. Delete pilot records on participant request and follow the published backup-retention policy.
